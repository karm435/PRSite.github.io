FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Parents Room Finder available on Android and iPhone?

Yes. Parents Room Finder is available for Android on Google Play and for iPhone on the App Store. Both apps are free, with no ads.

Is Parents Room Finder free to use?

Yes! Parents Room Finder is completely free to use. You get the interactive map, room details, photos, reviews, and the ability to save favourites and share rooms, at no cost. If you’d like to support the app you can, but it doesn’t unlock anything extra.

How accurate is the room information?

Room information is contributed by our community of parents, supplemented by our discovery tools. A room a parent adds shows “Verification needed” until three other parents have reviewed it. Details like opening hours and amenities may change, so please report anything that’s wrong from the room’s page and we’ll keep the information up to date.

Can I add rooms that aren’t listed?

Absolutely! We rely on our community to help map parent rooms across Australia. Tap Share a room in the app, drop a pin, pick the amenities the room has, and add up to three photos. Photos are checked as you add them. Your room shows on the map straight away, marked “Verification needed” until three other parents review it.

What countries are supported?

We’re currently focused on Australia, starting with major cities like Brisbane, Sydney, and Melbourne. We plan to expand to other countries based on community demand. Want us in your area? Let us know!

Is my data safe?

Yes. We take privacy seriously and comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we only collect what’s necessary, and we never sell your personal information. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
